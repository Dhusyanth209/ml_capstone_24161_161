Novelty Expression & Presentation (POWURPOINT) Synopsis

This page summarizes the research novelty, mathematical contributions, and the slide-by-slide synopsis ready for your Capstone PPT presentation.

1. Summary of Research Novelty

  • Formulation as Quantum RL: Unlike classical genetic algorithms or gradient-based optimization, GPA formulates quantum sensor design as a fully quantum reinforcement learning framework.
  • Justified Quantum Speedup: Uses Quantum Phase Estimation (QPE) for phase-value estimation and Grover policy amplification (QPY), providing an O(&sqrt;N) optimization speedup over classical brute-force grid search.
  • Joint Versus Sequential Allocation: Contrasted with GAQA, which sequentially allocates rotation budgets to individual gates, GPA searches over the complete policy parameter space jointly, avoiding suboptimal local minima (Q = 0.50 vs 0.45).
  • HISQ Gate Decomposition: Demonstrates successful translation from an ideal 2-qubit squeezed circuit (QFI = 1.0) to a 3-qubit controlled-V NISQ-friendly circuit (QFI = 0.5) without exponential decoherence penalties.
