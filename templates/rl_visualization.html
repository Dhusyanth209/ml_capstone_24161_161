Hyperparameter & Discretization Interpretation

Question: Where does RL training heuristically sample actions?

The agent represents actions as pairs of squeezing parameters (theta_1, theta_2). To attain the paper's amplification benchmark of k = 50 rotations, the policy space is discretized into a 64x64 grid (N=4096 candidates). QPE evaluates the value v_pi(s) for each candidate, and QPI executes Grover amplification to project the quantum state onto the optimal QFI subspace.
