Grover Policy Agent (GPA) for Quantum Sensor Circuits

Welcome to the research prototype reproducing Alomari & amp; Kumar (arxiv:2502.13755); GPA formulates the discovery of optimal quantum sensing circuits as a quantum reinforcement learning task, employing Quantum Phase Estimation (QPE) for value function estimation and Quantum Policy Iteration (QPI) via Grover amplitude amplification to discover optimal entanglement and phase parameters.

System Architecture & amp; Workflow

The prototype implements all mathematical components of the paper:

  • Quantum Sensor Circuits: Base 2-qubit circuit with squeezing matrix S(theta) and Simplified 3-qubit circuit with V and V_dagger controlled gates.
  • Quantum Phase Estimation: Evaluates value functions V_pi(s) across counting qubit registers.
  • Grover Policy Iteration: Amplifies the probability amplitude of states with optimal Quantum Fisher Information (QFI).
